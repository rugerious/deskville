      How Deskville works

      You sign in with a demo account that belongs to one council. Deskville answers from that council's published pages only, and shows you the pages it used. The assistant runs during demonstration sessions; when it is switched off, the page still works but questions can't be answered.

      Can I trust this?

      Yes, because it:

      • only uses Council's pages
      • links you to the real page
      • says when it doesn't know
      • never asks for bank details
