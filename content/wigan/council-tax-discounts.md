# Discounts, exemptions and reductions (Wigan)

## Single person discount
A bill assumes two or more adults live in the home. If you're the only adult, the bill can be cut by **25%**.

## Care leavers
Care leavers living in the borough pay nothing until they turn 25 if they are the only person on the bill (or only with their partner). If a care leaver shares with one other joint tenant, the other person gets the 25% single person discount until the care leaver turns 25. This also applies to care leavers from other English councils who now live in Wigan.

## Students
A home where everyone is a full-time student may not have to pay at all.

## Major repairs
A home that needs, or is undergoing, major structural repairs may get a 75% reduction for up to 12 months, with evidence of the work.

## Low income
Council Tax Reduction can lower the bill for people on low incomes.

## How do I apply?
Through the council's online discounts and exemptions form. Tell the council if your circumstances change.
