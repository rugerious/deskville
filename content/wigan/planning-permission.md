# Applying for planning permission (Wigan)

## Do I need permission?
Some work counts as **permitted development** and needs no application, but the council can remove those rights in some areas (an Article 4 direction). The Planning Portal's interactive guide helps you check. For a formal answer, apply for a **Lawful Development Certificate**.

## Can I get advice first?
Yes: pre-application advice gives you an in-principle view before you apply.

## How do I apply?
Online through the Planning Portal: choose the right form, describe the work, upload site and location plans and other documents, pay the fee, submit. The council doesn't give help with the Portal itself. Check the council's validation checklist first: an application left invalid for 14 days or more is returned, and the council keeps part of the fee.

## Fees
The planning fee is set nationally and calculated by the Portal. On top, the council charges a 2026/27 administrative fee: householder **£65**, advertisements £65, minor £90, small major £115, large major £285.

## After applying
Search and view applications on the council's planning pages.
