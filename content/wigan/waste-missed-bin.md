# Reporting a missed bin (Wigan)

## Before reporting
The council publishes a "missed streets" list, updated at 5pm on each collection day. If your street is on it, don't report: leave the bin out and it will be collected.

## When can I report?
After 8pm on your collection day (when the crews have finished), and no later than 48 hours after it.

## When the bin won't be collected again
- It was put out after 6am.
- It contained the wrong items or was too heavy: there will be a sticker on it. Take out the wrong items before your next collection.

## How do I report?
Use the online missed-bin form. Shared bins at flats have their own form. You should see a success message and receive a confirmation email; if you get neither, the report was probably outside the reporting window.

## What happens next?
Put the bin out before 6am from the following Monday; it will be collected on one of the days that week.
