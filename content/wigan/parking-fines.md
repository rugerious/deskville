# Parking fines (PCNs) and appeals (Wigan)

## What is a PCN?
A Penalty Charge Notice: the council's parking fine. It's a civil charge, so no criminal record and no points on your licence. The amount depends on the offence.

## How long do I have?
- Pay or challenge within **28 days**. Pay within **14 days** and the charge is **halved**.
- Unpaid after 28 days: a **Notice to Owner** goes to the registered keeper, who has another 28 days to pay or make representations.
- Ignore that, and a **Charge Certificate** adds **50%**; it must be paid within 14 days and can't be appealed.
- After that the debt can be registered at the County Court (court costs added), and enforcement agents may be used.

## How do I challenge it?
In writing: email parking@wigan.gov.uk (with your full postal address), or post to Parking Services, Wigan Council, PO Box 100, Wigan WN1 3DS. The council's cancellation policy explains when it may cancel a PCN, for example a valid permit that fell out of view, if you can show it. You can't appeal through the "report parking issues" form.

## How do I pay?
Online with a card; by automated phone line 01942 488280 (24 hours); cash at PayPoint; cash or cheque at a Post Office (take the PCN). Keep the receipt.
