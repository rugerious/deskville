# Moving home and other changes (Wigan)

## I'm moving into, within or out of the borough
Tell the council using the online change of address form.

## Someone has moved in or out
Use the change of circumstances form. If you become the only adult in the home, apply for the single person discount; if another adult moves in, the discount must stop.

## Someone has died
Report it with the change of circumstances form.

## Other changes
There are online forms to change your payment date, correct a name or billing date, and set up a Direct Debit. Landlords report tenancy changes through MyAccount; lenders, agents and solicitors report repossessions on a separate council tax page.
