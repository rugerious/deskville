# Taxi and private hire driver licence (Wigan)

## Who can apply?
You must be **over 21**, have held a full driving licence for at least **12 months**, be entitled to work in the UK and be a "fit and proper person".

## What do I send?
The completed application, payment receipt, medical form, a recent photo, proof of right to work, evidence of English (new applicants), three forms of ID for the DBS check, and a copy of your driving licence. If you lived abroad for more than 3 months in the last 5 years, a certificate of good conduct too. From **5 October 2026** new applicants must show English at Entry Level 3 or above.

## Criminal record check
Once your application is complete you're emailed a link to an online DBS application, to finish and pay within 7 days. The DBS Update Service (£15 a year, register within 30 days of the certificate) lets the council check you without a new certificate; drivers not on it are checked every six months.

## Training
Driver training is mandatory; missing it can lead to suspension or refusal, recorded on the national NR3S register.

## How do I apply and how long does it take?
Email the full application to the Licensing Team. The council aims to acknowledge within 5–10 working days and decide within 5 working days of all checks being complete. Renew up to 8 weeks before expiry (ideally at least 4). Fees are on the council's taxi licence fees page.
