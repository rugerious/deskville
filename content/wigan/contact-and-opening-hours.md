# Contacting the council and opening hours (Wigan)

## Online, any time
The council's website and MyAccount let you report problems, pay bills and apply for services 24/7.

## Phone
Main number: **01942 244991** (as listed by public directories; check the council's contact page for the right department).

## In person: Life Centres
- **Wigan Life Centre:** The Wiend, Wigan WN1 1NH (top of Millgate, opposite "The Face of Wigan" sculpture).
- **Leigh Life Centre:** Turnpike Centre, Civic Square, Market Street, Leigh WN7 1EB.
- Both open **Monday to Friday, 10am to 4pm**; closed at weekends.

Wigan Life Centre has self-service computers and Citizens Advice in the building.

## Town Hall
Library Street, Wigan WN1 1YN.

## Emergencies
The council's Emergencies page lists who to call out of hours.
