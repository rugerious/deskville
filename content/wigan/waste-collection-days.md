# Bin collection days and which bin is which (Wigan)

## How do I find my collection day?
Use the council's MyNeighbourhood service (apps.wigan.gov.uk/MyNeighbourhood): search for your address to see your next collection dates and download a printable bin calendar.

## Which bin is which, and how often is it emptied?
- **Black bin:** general waste that can't be recycled, including nappies, pet waste and cat litter. Every 3 weeks.
- **Brown bin:** mixed recycling: cans, tins, aerosols, foil, glass bottles and jars, plastic bottles, pots, tubs, bags and films, and cartons. Every 3 weeks.
- **Blue bin (or white sack):** paper and cardboard. Every 3 weeks.
- **Green bin or caddy:** food and garden waste together. Food can go in loose. Every 2 weeks.

Batteries, vapes and electrical items never go in a kerbside bin.

## When should I put my bins out?
Not before 6pm the evening before, and at the collection point by 6:30am on collection day. (The missed-bin page uses 6am, so 6am is the safe time.) Take bins back in by midnight on collection day.

## Extra bags next to the bin?
Not collected: anything left beside or on top of a bin stays there.
