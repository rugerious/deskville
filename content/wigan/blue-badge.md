# Blue Badge (disabled parking) (Wigan)

## What does it let me do?
Park free in some pay and display car parks, and on single and double yellow lines in most streets for up to three hours.

## How do I apply?
Online, for yourself, someone else, or an organisation that transports disabled people (taxi firms can't apply). The form checks eligibility first. Every application is treated as new, even if you had a badge before. You'll need proof of identity, proof of address, proof of benefit if relevant, and your current badge details if re-applying. Some applicants are asked to see an independent occupational therapist at Wigan or Leigh Life Centre.

## Cost
If a first application is approved you may pay **£10** and visit a Life Centre for your photo and document check.

## Renewing
Badges last **three years** and renewing is your responsibility. The earliest you can re-apply is **6 weeks** before it expires.

## Need help?
Wigan and Leigh Life Centres can help you apply, take your photo and check documents.
