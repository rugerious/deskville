# Household waste recycling centres (Wigan)

## Where are they?
- **Kirkless:** Makerfield Way, Higher Ince, WN2 2PR
- **Slag Lane:** Leigh, WA3 1BZ
- **Chanters:** Chanters Industrial Estate, Atherton, M46 9BP

## Opening hours
- Summer (from the last Sunday in March): every day, 8am to 7:45pm.
- Winter (from the last Sunday in October): every day, 9am to 5:45pm.
- Open on bank holidays; closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. Sites may close early if queues build near closing time.

## Do I need a permit?
Not in a car. Vans and other vehicles carrying your household waste need a permit, ordered online and emailed within about two hours, valid for one visit within seven days; each household gets 12 a year. Hire vehicles need a free permit plus the hire paperwork and a driving licence.

## What can I take?
Recycling, appliances (fridges and freezers emptied), batteries, engine oil, wood, plasterboard, garden waste, rubble, paint, gas bottles, up to four car tyres, mattresses, carpets and textiles. Asbestos, pesticides and chemicals only at Kirkless (asbestos by appointment).

## What can't I take?
Petrol, diesel, trade waste and sharps.
