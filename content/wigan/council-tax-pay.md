# How to pay council tax (Wigan)

## Ways to pay
- **Direct Debit:** choose the 1st, 6th, 15th, 21st or 27th of the month.
- **Online:** by debit or credit card, any time. Your account reference starts with 4.
- **Phone:** automated card payment line, 24 hours: 01942 489006 (no American Express).
- **Bank transfer, online banking or standing order:** quote your 12-digit reference starting with 4.
- **In person:** cash or card at a Post Office, or cash at a PayPoint, using a bill with a barcode.

## How many instalments?
Normally 10 monthly payments; 12 if you ask. If you start mid-year, the bill is spread over the months left.

## Checking your account
MyAccount shows your latest balance and payments.

## Struggling to pay?
Contact the council as early as possible; its corporate debt policy explains the help available.
