# Reporting fly-tipping (Wigan)

## How do I report it?
Residents report fly-tipping on public land through MyAccount; people who don't live in the borough can use the council's online form without an account. Abandoned shopping trolleys can be reported through the Trolleywise app or to the store.

## Is it dangerous?
It can be: dumped waste may include clinical or hazardous items. Don't handle it.

## What are the penalties?
Fly-tipping is a criminal offence. In a magistrates' court the penalty can reach a £50,000 fine, 12 months in prison, or both; in the Crown Court, an unlimited fine and up to five years in prison.

## My responsibilities (duty of care)
Keep your waste on your own property until it can be disposed of properly: a recycling centre or a bulky collection. If you pay someone to take it away, check they're a registered waste carrier on the Environment Agency's public register and ask for a receipt showing what they took and where it went.
