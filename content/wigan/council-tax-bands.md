# Council tax bands and how much they cost (2026/27) (Wigan)

## How is my band decided?
Every home is in one of eight bands, A to H, based on what it would have sold for on 1 April 1991, not what it is worth today. The Valuation Office Agency (VOA), not the council, sets the band. You can check a band, or ask for it to be reviewed, on GOV.UK.

## How much is each band in 2026/27?
For most of the borough (areas without a parish council):

| Band | A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| £ per year | 1,434.35 | 1,673.42 | 1,912.47 | 2,151.54 | 2,629.65 | 3,107.77 | 3,585.89 | 4,303.08 |

Two parishes pay slightly more because of their parish charge: **Haigh** (Band D £2,210.99) and **Shevington** (Band D £2,180.42).

## Where do these figures come from?
The council's statutory notice of the 2026/27 council tax, published in March 2026. The amount on your own bill also reflects any discounts or reductions you get.
