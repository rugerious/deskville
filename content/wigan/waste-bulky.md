# Bulky waste collections (Wigan)

## What is it?
The council collects large household, garden and electrical items from outside your home. You book online through MyAccount (register first if you don't have an account).

## How much does it cost?
Every property gets **two free collections a year**, each of up to five items. Extra collections cost **£27** for up to five items. Some items count as more than one: a table with up to four chairs counts as two; a bed base, headboard and mattress count as three.

## What can I book?
Furniture (sofas, wardrobes, beds, mattresses, tables, carpets, bikes), garden items (lawnmowers, garden furniture, barbecues without the gas bottle, wheelbarrows) and appliances (cookers, emptied fridges and freezers, washing machines, TVs, computers).

## What can't go?
Black bags and household rubbish, bathroom suites, doors, wood, worktops, fence panels, sheds, soil, garden waste, gas bottles, hazardous or clinical waste, car parts and trade waste. Take these to a recycling centre.

## On the day
Items outside, covered and easy to reach by 6am. Crews don't go inside. Only the booked items are taken.

## Booking window
Slots open up to five weeks ahead. Cancel or change a booking in MyAccount.
