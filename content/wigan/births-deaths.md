# Registering a birth or a death (Wigan)

## Registering a birth
- Within **42 days** of the birth, by **appointment**, in the district where the baby was born.
- Married parents: one parent can register. Unmarried parents who want the father named: both must attend.
- Certificates are paid by card only (no cash).

## Registering a death
You don't need to contact the register office: **the registrars contact the next of kin** to arrange an appointment, once the Medical Examiner or coroner has sent the paperwork.

## Where?
Wigan Life Centre (North Site), The Wiend, Wigan WN1 1NH, or Leigh Turnpike Centre, Civic Square, Market Street, Leigh WN7 1EB.

## Tell Us Once
When registering a death you can use Tell Us Once to inform several government and council services in one go.
