# Parking permits (Wigan)

## What permits are there?
Resident, business and contract parking permits. Apply for or renew them online through the council's permits page.

## Do I need one?
Only if you live or work in a **Controlled Parking Zone (CPZ)**, an area where all on-street parking is controlled. The council's "Find my parking zone" page lists the zones in Wigan and Leigh; each zone has its own regulations.

## How much does it cost?
The permits page doesn't list prices; check the application form for your zone.

## Problem parking near my home
Residents can report dropped-kerb obstruction, parking outside schools, pavement and verge parking, and parking against traffic restrictions, through MyAccount (non-residents have a separate form). For an ongoing problem you can attach a 14-day diary sheet. The council can only act where a parking restriction exists.
