# Alcohol licences: personal, premises and temporary events (Wigan)

## Personal licence (for a person)
Lets you sell alcohol, or authorise its sale, under a premises licence. You must be 18+, hold a recognised licensing qualification, and send a DBS certificate dated within one month, a convictions declaration, proof of right to work and two passport photos (one endorsed). The fee is **£37**. The council aims to decide within 21 days. Personal licences don't need renewing and work anywhere in England and Wales.

## Premises licence (for a place)
Needed to sell alcohol, put on regulated entertainment (plays, films, music, dance, indoor sport) or serve hot food or drink between 11pm and 5am. Apply online through GOV.UK. New and variation applications must be advertised with a notice at the premises and in the press. A new application also needs the consent of the designated premises supervisor. There is an annual fee.

## Temporary Event Notice (TEN)
For a one-off event: up to **499 people** at a time and up to **168 hours**. Given by an individual aged 18+. Standard TENs need at least **10 clear working days**' notice; late TENs 5 to 9 working days (exceptional cases only). Per year: 5 TENs without a personal licence, 50 with one (of which 2 or 10 can be late). A premises can host up to 15 TENs, 21 days in total. Police and environmental protection have two working days to object.
