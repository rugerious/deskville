# Garden waste (Wigan)

## Which bin?
Garden waste goes in the **green bin**, together with food waste. It is emptied every 2 weeks.

## What can go in?
Grass cuttings, leaves, twigs and branches narrower than 15cm.

## What can't?
Thick branches (15cm or more), soil, rubble, plastic bags, cardboard, pet waste and cat litter.

## Too much garden waste for the bin?
Take it to a household waste recycling centre. A car needs no permit for small loads; very large amounts from big gardens need a garden waste permit, ordered online.

## Is it free?
The council's bin pages describe the green bin as part of the standard service and don't mention a charge. Check with the council if in doubt.
