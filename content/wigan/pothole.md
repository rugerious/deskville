# Reporting a pothole (Wigan)

## How do I report one?
Through the council's **Report It** app, with an accurate location and a photo.

## What counts?
There's no national definition. The council normally repairs road potholes deeper than **40mm**.

## How quickly is it dealt with?
Reports are normally investigated within **10 working days**. Repairs are prioritised by depth, location and how busy the road is: standard repairs usually within 14 or 28 working days, high priority within 24 hours, emergencies within 2 hours.

## Will I be told?
You'll be told whether the council will repair it, but not automatically when the repair is finished.

## My car was damaged
Write to the Insurance Department, Legal Services, Town Hall, Library Street, Wigan, WN1 1YN. Every claim is investigated and answered in writing, but the council is only liable if it failed to find or repair the defect under its highway inspection policy, and most claims are rejected.
