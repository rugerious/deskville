# School admissions (starting school in September 2027) (Wigan)

## Reception (children born 1 September 2022 – 31 August 2023)
- Applications open by **30 September 2026**.
- Closing date: **15 January 2027**.
- National offer day: **16 April 2027**. Online applicants get an email from 9am; paper applicants get a letter.

You must apply even if your child is at the school's nursery or a brother or sister already goes there.

## Secondary (Year 7)
Apply in the autumn of Year 6; the national closing date is 31 October and offers are made on 1 March.

## How do I apply?
Online, on the council's school admissions pages. Some schools also need a supplementary form returned to the school by the closing date.

## Help
School Organisation Team: **01942 489013**, schoolplaces@wigan.gov.uk.

## Didn't get the school you wanted?
The council's appeals page explains how to appeal.
