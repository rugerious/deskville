# Reporting fly-tipping (Bury)

## How do I report it?
Use the council's online fly-tipping form for ordinary waste. Say where it is, what kind of waste and roughly how much, and anything you saw about who dumped it.

## Hazardous waste?
Don't touch it. Phone the council: 0161 253 5353 (Monday to Friday, 8:45am to 5pm) or 0161 253 6606 out of hours.

## How quickly is it cleared?
On council land (roads, pavements, parks, cemeteries, car parks) usually within about ten working days; hazardous or complicated waste can take longer. Private land, unadopted roads and business premises are normally the owner's responsibility, though enforcement officers may take action. Reports about Metrolink lines, motorways and rivers are passed to the body responsible.

## What are the penalties?
Fines of up to £50,000 and prison; vehicles used can be seized and crushed. Fixed penalty notices can be paid online.

## My responsibilities (duty of care)
Make sure your household waste is disposed of legally. Before paying anyone to take it, check their waste carrier licence (the Environment Agency has a free online check), get their full contact details and ask where the waste will go. Failing this duty can mean a fine of up to £5,000 in a magistrates' court, or unlimited in the Crown Court.
