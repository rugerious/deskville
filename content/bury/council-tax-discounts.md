# Discounts, exemptions and reductions (Bury)

## Single person discount
If you're the only adult in the home, you can apply for the single person discount (the standard reduction in England is 25%). If a second adult moves in, or a child turns 18, it must be cancelled.

## Other discounts
- Second adult rebate, for people of pension age without a partner.
- Disabled person discount, for homes where a disabled adult or child lives.
- Discounts linked to some roles: apprentices, carers and care workers, special constables, students and student nurses.
- Care leaver discount and occupied property discount.

## Low income
Council Tax Support can reduce the bill; claim online.

## How do I apply?
The person responsible for the bill applies. Each discount has its own page and form on the council's website.
