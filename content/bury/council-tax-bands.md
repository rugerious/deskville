# Council tax bands and how much they cost (2026/27) (Bury)

## How is my band decided?
Every home is in one of eight bands, A to H, based on its value on 1 April 1991, not today. The Valuation Office Agency (VOA), not the council, sets the band. Check your band on GOV.UK.

## How much is each band in 2026/27?

| Band | 1991 value | £ per year |
|---|---|---|
| A | up to £40,000 | 1,703.43 |
| B | £40,001 to £52,000 | 1,987.34 |
| C | £52,001 to £68,000 | 2,271.24 |
| D | £68,001 to £88,000 | 2,555.15 |
| E | £88,001 to £120,000 | 3,122.96 |
| F | £120,001 to £160,000 | 3,690.77 |
| G | £160,001 to £320,000 | 4,258.58 |
| H | over £320,000 | 5,110.30 |

The amount on your own bill also reflects any discounts or reductions you get.
