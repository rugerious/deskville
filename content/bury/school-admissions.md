# School admissions (starting school in September 2027) (Bury)

## Reception (children born 1 September 2022 – 31 August 2023)
- Applications open **1 November 2026**.
- Closing date: **15 January 2027**.

You must apply even if an older child already attends the school or nursery.

## Secondary (Year 7)
Children move to secondary school in the September after their 11th birthday. Applications open on 1 September of Year 6 and close on **31 October**; applying late can seriously reduce the chance of a preferred school. Feeder primary schools are not part of Bury's admission criteria.

## How do I apply?
Online through the council's admissions portal, or with a paper form sent to admissions@bury.gov.uk or to the Admissions Team, 3 Knowsley Place, Duke Street, Bury BL9 0EJ.

## Help or changes after applying
Admissions Team: **0161 253 6474**, admissions@bury.gov.uk.
