# Reporting a pothole (Bury)

## How do I report one?
Online, on the council's "Report a pot hole" page. Give the street, where the pothole is, its shape and size, the nearest landmark and postcode, and a photo if you can.

## How quickly is it dealt with?
Most reports are inspected within **10 working days**. Inspectors judge the risk: emergencies are attended within **2 hours**, most minor repairs are done within **28 days** of the inspection, and very low-risk defects can wait up to six months. Every adopted street is also inspected at least once a year.

## How is it repaired?
Either a quick fill (compacted, sometimes a stopgap) or a permanent repair (the area is cut out, cleaned, filled and sealed). The council also uses a spray-injection patcher that can fix over 100 potholes a day.

## My car was damaged
Write to the Insurance Department, Bury Council, Town Hall, Knowsley Street, Bury BL9 0SW. Every claim is investigated and answered in writing, but the council is only liable if it failed to find or repair the defect under its inspection policy, and most claims are rejected.
