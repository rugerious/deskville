# Moving home and other changes (Bury)

## Moving into Bury
Register for council tax at your new address. If you weren't paying before (for example, living with family), use the "register for council tax" form.

## Moving within Bury
Tell the council: you're billed up to your move-out date, then at the new address. Any overpayment moves to your new account.

## Moving out of Bury
Tell the council: you're billed up to your move-out date and any overpayment is refunded. Moving abroad has its own form.

## Buying or selling
Tell the council when you buy a property (even if you won't live there) and when you sell one.

## Someone moves out, or a death
Report it. If you become the only adult, you may qualify for the single person discount. If the person who died was registered through Tell Us Once, the council is told automatically. An empty home of someone who died is exempt until six months after probate.

## How do I tell the council?
Online forms on the change of circumstances page, or phone 0161 253 5000. Council tax is worked out day by day, so tell the council the exact date.

## Also remember
Update your electoral registration, any Housing Benefit or Council Tax Support claim, and your bin collection details.
