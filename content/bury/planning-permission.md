# Applying for planning permission (Bury)

## Do I need permission?
Some projects are **permitted development** and need no full application, but restrictions on your property can still require consent. Use the Planning Portal's guides and interactive house. For a formal answer, or to confirm a use is lawful (useful when selling), apply for a **Lawful Development Certificate**.

## Advice first
Pre-application advice gives the council's opinion before you apply.

## How do I apply?
Online through a digital submission platform (such as the Planning Portal), using the council's validation checklists for the documents needed. The fee is calculated online and must be paid before the council receives the application.

## How long does it take?
National targets: usually **8 weeks** for minor applications and **13 weeks** for major ones; extensions can be agreed.

## Neighbours and comments
Neighbours may be sent letters. **Anyone** can view plans and comment online within the statutory **21 days**; comments are public and summarised in the officer's report.

## Refused?
Resubmit addressing the reasons, or appeal to the Planning Inspectorate. Neighbours have no right of appeal against an approval.
