# Reporting a missed bin (Bury)

## Before reporting
The council's missed-bins page lists streets where collections were missed that day. If your whole street (or part of it) was missed, you don't need to report it. You also don't need to report if your bin wasn't full and you can wait for the next collection.

## When can I report?
After 5pm on your collection day, and by **1pm the next day**.

## When the bin won't count as missed
It wasn't out by 7am, it was overfilled so the lid didn't close, or it held the wrong items.

## How do I report?
Use the online form for the colour of the missed bin. Communal bins (flats) are handled through the communal bins service.

## What happens next?
- Grey, green or blue bin: the council posts you official bags to use until your next collection.
- Brown bin: the council comes back within 5 working days.
- Assisted collections: the council comes back within 3 working days.
