# Bin collection days and which bin is which (Bury)

## How do I find my collection day?
Enter your postcode and choose your address on the council's "bin collection days and alerts" page. You can also sign up there for email reminders and service updates.

## Which bin is which, and how often is it emptied?
- **Grey bin:** general waste that can't be recycled. Every 3 weeks.
- **Green bin:** paper and cardboard. Every 3 weeks.
- **Blue bin:** mixed recycling (plastic bottles, glass bottles and jars, cans, aerosols, foil). Every 3 weeks.
- **Brown bin:** garden and food waste. Every 2 weeks.

The council's "bins information" pages list exactly what each bin takes.

## When should I put my bins out?
At the kerbside or your collection point by **7am** on collection day. Bins that are overfilled (lid not closed) or contain the wrong items won't be emptied.
