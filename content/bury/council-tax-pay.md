# How to pay council tax (Bury)

## Ways to pay
- **Direct Debit:** choose the 1st, 5th, 15th or 25th of the month. Set it up online (joint accounts needing two signatures use a paper form).
- **Online:** debit card, credit card or PayPal.
- **Phone:** automated line, 24 hours: 0300 456 0542 (you need your account number). Without it, call 0161 253 5656, weekdays 8:45am to 5pm.
- **Bank transfer or standing order:** quote your 8-digit account number starting with 4.
- **In person:** at a Post Office or PayPoint.

## How many instalments?
10 monthly (April to January), 12 monthly (April to March), two payments (April and October) or one payment in April. You can ask to change the date or the number of instalments.

## Struggling to pay?
Contact the council early. It will ask for an income and expenditure assessment to consider a payment plan. Even after a court summons it may help in exceptional cases. If your debt is with an enforcement agent, pay them directly.
