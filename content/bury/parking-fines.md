# Parking fines (PCNs) and appeals (Bury)

## How much is it?
**£50 or £70**, depending on the offence, **halved to £25 or £35** if paid within 14 days. Tickets are issued on the council's behalf by its enforcement contractor, NSL Services Ltd.

## How do I pay?
Online with the 10-digit PCN number (starting "BC") and your registration, or by phone, 24 hours, on 0344 870 5545.

## How do I challenge it? Three stages
1. **Informal appeal**, within 14 days of the ticket. If it's rejected, you get another 14 days to pay at the half rate.
2. **Formal appeal** to the council. After 28 days the case moves to the council's Parking Services, who ask for the full amount; you can then appeal formally.
3. **Traffic Penalty Tribunal**, independent, once the council has rejected your formal appeal in writing.

Stages 1 and 2 use the same online form; the tribunal is at tpt.gov.uk.

## Important
**Paying closes the case.** If you pay while an appeal is open, the appeal ends.
