# Household waste and recycling centres (Bury)

## Where are they?
- **Bury:** Every Street, Bury, BL9 5BE
- **Radcliffe:** Cemetery Road, Radcliffe, M26 4FT

Bury residents can also use any recycling centre in Greater Manchester.

## Opening hours
Every day: 8am to 8pm in summer, 8am to 6pm in winter. Closed only on 25 December and 1 January.

## Do I need a permit?
Not in a car. Vans, hire vans, pick-ups and twin-axle trailers need a permit, applied for through the Recycle for Greater Manchester website. Vans longer than 5.3m or heavier than 3.5 tonnes can't use any Greater Manchester centre. The Bury site has a 2m height barrier; taller vehicles use the weighbridge.

## How many visits?
Number plates are read by cameras. Per year: 52 visits by car (or car with a single-axle trailer), 18 with a double-axle trailer, 18 by van or pick-up.

## What can I take?
Household waste and recycling only, sorted before you arrive. No business or trade waste, and no asbestos.
