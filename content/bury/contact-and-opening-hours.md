# Contacting the council and opening hours (Bury)

## Online, any time
The council website lets you pay, apply and report most things online.

## Phone
Main council number: **0161 253 5000**.

## In person
**Bury Town Hall**, Knowsley Street, Bury BL9 0SW. Services there include the Register Office (Monday to Friday, 9am to 4:30pm, by appointment) and collection points such as temporary parking permits.

## Useful direct lines
- Council tax payments (automated, 24 hours): 0300 456 0542
- Bulky waste for Council Tax benefit recipients, and hazardous fly-tipping (weekdays 8:45am to 5pm): 0161 253 5353
- Out-of-hours hazardous waste: 0161 253 6606
- School admissions: 0161 253 6474
