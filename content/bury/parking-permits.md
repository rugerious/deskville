# Parking permits (Bury)

## Who can get a resident permit?
People living in a **Resident Parking Scheme** zone (streets near town centres, hospitals and Metrolink stations). Permits cover your own vehicle and your visitors. Businesses inside a zone can get a business permit. **No new resident schemes are being accepted** at the moment; existing ones carry on.

## How do I apply?
Online, with proof of vehicle ownership (insurance or log book) and proof you live in the zone (council tax, business rates or utility bill). If you can't upload them, take them to Bury Town Hall.

## Temporary permits for an event
For a party or funeral, up to 10 permits per event at **£3.50 each**. Apply at least 48 hours ahead and collect from Bury Town Hall reception.

## Changes
Report a house move, new vehicle or lost permit online. Return the old permit before a new one is issued. Permits can't be passed on or altered.

## Contract parking in council car parks
From £245 for 3 months (Monday to Friday) to £1,240 for 12 months (Monday to Saturday).
