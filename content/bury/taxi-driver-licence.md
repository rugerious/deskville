# Taxi and private hire driver licence (Bury)

## Who can apply?
You must be **over 18** and have held a full UK driving licence for at least **12 months** (provisional licences and pass certificates don't count).

## What do I need?
- An **enhanced DBS** certificate on the DBS Update Service (sign up within 30 days of issue).
- A **DVLA group 2** medical certificate, less than four months old when the licence is issued (best done after the knowledge test).
- Proof of passing the practical taxi driver assessment and **safeguarding** training.
- English and maths qualifications (GCSE, Functional Skills level 1 or 2, etc.), or pass the council's communication and numeracy assessment.
- Right to work (UK passport or share code); a certificate of good conduct if you haven't lived in the UK for the last five years.
- Driving licence with DVLA share code, photo, HMRC tax share code and National Insurance number.

## Fee and process
**£280**, paid with the online application, covering the licence and the **knowledge test**. A licensing officer then contacts you to book the test. If you pass and meet all the criteria, the council aims to issue the licence within **28 days**. Licences last **three years**.
