# Alcohol licences: personal, premises and temporary events (Bury)

## Premises licence (for a place)
Needed to sell alcohol, put on regulated entertainment or serve hot food or drink between 11pm and 5am (pubs, off-licences, supermarkets, takeaways). It lasts for the life of the premises and can be varied later.

## How do I apply?
Online through GOV.UK. Applicants must be 18+ with an interest in the property. Send the fee, an operating schedule (activities and hours) and a clear premises plan (two copies at 1:100 showing exits, fire equipment, toilets, kitchens). For alcohol you need the premises supervisor's consent, and **a personal licence first**.

## Advertising and decision
Display a blue A4 notice at the premises for **28 days** from the day after applying, and advertise in the **Bury Times** within 10 working days. With no objections after 28 days the licence is granted; with objections a councillor panel holds a hearing (decision within 42 days). Refusals can be appealed to the Magistrates' Court within 21 days. There is an application fee and an annual fee.

## Changing hands
If a new licensee takes over, the licence must be transferred and name the new designated premises supervisor; otherwise it is void and sales must stop.

## Personal licences and temporary events
Both are applied for through GOV.UK. A Temporary Event Notice may suit an occasional event at a community centre, school or church hall.
