# Blue Badge (disabled parking) (Bury)

## What is it?
Parking concessions for people with severe mobility problems, or who can't make journeys without overwhelming psychological distress. It works across the UK.

## Who is eligible?
Some people aged over three are automatically eligible (for example through certain benefits); others are assessed, sometimes by a mobility assessor, which takes longer.

## How do I apply or renew?
Online on the council's Blue Badge page. Re-apply some weeks before your badge runs out, but **not more than 12 weeks** before. If approved, the badge comes by post.

## Using it
The badge holder must be in the vehicle, as driver or passenger, when it is used.

## Lost, stolen or damaged
Request a replacement online and return a damaged badge to Blue Badges, Bury Council, Town Hall, Knowsley Street, Bury BL9 0SW. If it expires within 3 months, re-apply instead.

## No longer needed
Tell the council (for example if the holder has died or moved away). Registering a death with Tell Us Once cancels the badge automatically.
