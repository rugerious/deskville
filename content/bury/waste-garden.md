# Garden waste (Bury)

## Which bin?
Garden waste goes in the **brown bin**, together with food waste. It is emptied every 2 weeks.

## What if the brown bin is missed?
Report it after 5pm on collection day and before 1pm the next day; the council returns within 5 working days.

## Too much garden waste for the bin?
Take it to the household waste and recycling centres in Bury (Every Street) or Radcliffe (Cemetery Road). Cars need no permit.

## Is it free?
The council's collection pages describe the brown bin as part of the service but don't state a charge. Check with the council if in doubt.
