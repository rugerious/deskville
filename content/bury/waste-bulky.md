# Bulky waste collections (Bury)

## How do I book?
Online, through the council's bulky waste booking form. Dates are often available within a week, and you can book up to three weeks ahead.

## How much does it cost?
**£49** per collection, for up to five items. The charge isn't refundable, including if you cancel or have fewer items on the day. Some items count as several (a double wardrobe counts as four). You can't add items after booking.

## Is there a discount?
People receiving Council Tax benefit can have one half-price collection a year (up to five items), booked by phone on 0161 253 5353.

## What can I book?
Mattresses, sofas, beds, tables, chairs, carpets, bikes, TVs, washing machines, fridges, freezers and many other household items.

## What can't go?
Bagged household waste, fence panels and posts, fitted wardrobes and kitchens, worktops, sheds, garages, gas bottles, glass items including shower screens, mirrors, pianos and other instruments, large gym equipment, double-extension ladders, window frames and large dog kennels.

## Where do I leave items?
Whole (not broken up), dry and covered, at your usual bin collection point, front garden or driveway. Crews don't collect from back gardens, yards, sheds, garages or inside homes and flats.
