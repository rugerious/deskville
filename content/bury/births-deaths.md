# Registering a birth or a death (Bury)

## Registering a birth
- Within **42 days**, by **appointment**, at the register office for the district where the baby was born.
- Bury Register Office runs a strict appointment system and only books appointments for people living in Bury.
- Copy birth certificates after registration are ordered from Manchester or Bolton register offices; allow four weeks.

## Registering a death
- Must be registered within **five days** of the register office receiving the paperwork.
- The Medical Examiner sends the medical certificate (MCCD) straight to the register office. **Only book an appointment once the doctor or hospital confirms it has been emailed.**
- Certificates cost **£12.50** each.
- You can give the details at another register office in England or Wales by declaration.

## Where?
Bury Register Office, Town Hall, Knowsley Street, Bury BL9 0SW. Open Monday to Friday, 9am to 4:30pm; closed at weekends.
